Privacy, in plain language

Last updated: 2 September 2026. Gridsong is designed for classrooms and does not require an account to compose.

What stays on your device

Your current song and the teacher access key for the last class board are stored in that browser’s local storage. The sample demo uses separate keys beginning with demo:gridsong., so it does not read or write your real song. Student compositions can also be saved as links or WAV/MIDI files on the device.

What a class pass or song link contains

A student class pass contains an opaque board reference, a submit-only key, and an expiry. It works on another device but cannot read the teacher’s private board. A song link carries the song title, notes, tempo, scale, length, and instrument choices in the URL itself.

Anyone who receives a pass or song link can use the capability or read the song data. Do not use full names or include sensitive information in song titles.

What the class gallery stores

When a student presses “Send to class gallery,” Gridsong stores the classroom nickname, composition, submission time, expiry, and security hashes needed to operate that board. The submission request contains only the nickname, composition, and submit capability.

The compose and gallery forms do not ask for email addresses or account credentials.

Boards and their submissions expire after 90 days and are rejected immediately. Expired records are removed in bounded cleanup during later board creation. Teachers can remove a submission sooner.

What we do not do

The delivered app sets no cookies and loads no third-party fonts or scripts.

Children and schools

Teachers should ask students to use classroom nicknames, initials, or assigned aliases. Schools remain responsible for their own policies and consent requirements.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact the site operator through sociobot.in.
