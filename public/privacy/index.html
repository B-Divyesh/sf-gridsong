Privacy, in plain language

Last updated: 27 August 2026. Gridsong is designed for classrooms and does not require an account.

What stays on your device

Your current song, gallery codes, student nicknames, and gallery submissions are stored in your browser’s local storage. Gallery data expires by policy after 90 days and is removed the next time that gallery is opened. You can remove submissions in the gallery or clear the site’s storage in browser settings.

What a link or ticket contains

A song link carries the song title, notes, tempo, scale, length, and instrument choices in the URL itself. A submission ticket carries that song plus the nickname the student entered. Anyone who receives a link or ticket can read and play that data, so do not use full names or include sensitive information in song titles.

What we collect

The app includes no advertising, behavioral analytics, cookies, fingerprinting, third-party fonts, or third-party scripts. The hosting service may process basic request logs for security and reliability. Gridsong does not ask for email addresses, real names, audio recordings, microphone access, or precise location.

Children and schools

Teachers should ask students to use classroom nicknames, initials, or assigned aliases. Gridsong stores only the nickname and composition data needed for the classroom activity. Schools remain responsible for their own policies and consent requirements.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact the site operator through sociobot.in.
