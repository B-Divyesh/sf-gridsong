Privacy, in plain language

Last updated: 2 September 2026. Gridsong is designed for classrooms and does not require an account to compose.

What stays on your device

Your current song and the teacher access key for the last class board are stored in that browser’s local storage. The sample demo uses separate keys beginning with demo:gridsong., so it does not read or write your real song. Student compositions can also be saved as links or WAV/MIDI files on the device.

What a student class pass or song link contains

A student class pass contains a board ID, a key that can only send songs, and an expiry. It works on another device but cannot read the teacher’s private board. A song link carries the title, notes, tempo, scale, length, and sound choices in its address.

Anyone who receives a student class pass can send a song. Anyone with a song link can read its song data. Do not use full names or include sensitive information in song titles.

What the class gallery stores

When a student sends a song, the gallery stores the nickname, song, submission time, expiry, and protected key checks. It never stores usable class or teacher keys. The request contains only the student class pass, nickname, and song.

The compose and gallery forms do not ask for email addresses or account credentials.

Boards and submissions close after 90 days. Expired records are deleted in small batches when a new board is created. Teachers can remove a submission sooner.

What we do not do

The delivered app sets no cookies and loads no third-party fonts or scripts.

Children and schools

Teachers should ask students to use classroom nicknames, initials, or assigned aliases. Schools remain responsible for their own policies and consent requirements.

Questions
