Privacy, in plain language

Last updated: 27 August 2026. Gridsong is designed for classrooms and does not require an account.

What stays on your device

Your current song and teacher class board, including student nicknames and submitted songs, are stored in that browser’s local storage. Teacher-board data expires by policy after 90 days and is removed the next time that board is opened. You can remove submissions in the board or clear the site’s storage in browser settings.

What a class pass, link, or ticket contains

A student class pass contains an opaque board reference and an expiry only. It works on another device but does not expose or load the teacher’s private board. A song link carries the song title, notes, tempo, scale, length, and instrument choices in the URL itself. A GS2T submission ticket carries its destination board reference, song, and the nickname the student entered. These values are carried directly between people or devices; they are not uploaded to Gridsong. Anyone who receives a link or ticket can read and play that data, so do not use full names or include sensitive information in song titles.

What we collect

The app includes no accounts, advertising, behavioral analytics, cookies, fingerprinting, third-party fonts, third-party scripts, or server-side gallery storage. The hosting service may process basic request logs for security and reliability. Gridsong does not ask for email addresses, real names, audio recordings, microphone access, or precise location.

Children and schools

Teachers should ask students to use classroom nicknames, initials, or assigned aliases. Gridsong stores only the nickname and composition data needed for the classroom activity on the teacher’s device. Schools remain responsible for their own policies and consent requirements.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact the site operator through sociobot.in.
